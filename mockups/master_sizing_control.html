Master sizing control — one capital dial, per-method intensity

The sidebar asks for one currency number that never changes meaning: the capital behind the book, W. Each sizing method adds only a dimensionless dial — R:R for fixed loss, λ for Kelly — and a live readout translates every choice back into dollars-at-risk as a % of W. Flip the method below and watch what changes (and what doesn't).

1Set capital once

W lives in the sidebar, persists across trades and methods. Replaces the hard-coded LINEAR_NOTIONAL.

2Pick the method

Fixed loss or Kelly. The currency input doesn't move or change meaning — only the dial below it swaps.

3Tune the dial

A unitless knob per method: required R:R (or type dollar risk — R:R back-solves), fractional-Kelly λ.

4Read the equivalents

Dollars-at-risk and % of W, stated the same way in both modes. Variants inherit the sizing automatically.

Fixed loss — mechanics

stop% = move ÷ R:R  →  loss $ = W × stop%  →  Nᵢ = min(loss$ ÷ max_lossᵢ, 10·W)

  • W acts as a linear-equivalent notional: risk what a delta-1 position of W would lose at its R:R-implied stop.
  • Every variant carries the same dollar max loss — the equaliser.
  • Risk scales with trade geometry (wider target → wider stop → more dollars). Conviction has no channel here; the dollar-risk override is the manual escape hatch.

Kelly — mechanics

x*ᵢ = argmax Σ p·ln(1 + x·πᵢ)  →  Nᵢ = min(λ·x*ᵢ·W, 10·W)

  • W is the whole bankroll for this trade, myopically; the log barrier caps worst modeled loss below 100% of W.
  • Each variant gets its own optimal size — nothing is equalised; conviction enters through the elicited distribution.
  • Worst case is evaluated on the distribution's ±4σ bins — tail beyond that is censored (same caveat as the scenario-grid loss fix).

What changes vs today

  • New: the W input (promotes the hard-coded LINEAR_NOTIONAL = 100 to a PM-set sidebar value — both methods already scale linearly off it, so the engine needs no re-plumbing).
  • New: the equivalents readout, and the dollar-risk ⇄ R:R back-solve in fixed-loss mode.
  • Moved: the R:R slider and λ already exist — they relocate under the method toggle as the "intensity" dials.
  • Unchanged: sizing math, caps (10·W notional; net-credit fixed at 10·W), variant ranking.
